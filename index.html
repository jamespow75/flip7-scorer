How a round is scored
  1. Add up your number cards.
  2. Double it if you have the ×2 card.
  3. Add the + modifier cards (they are never doubled).
  4. Add 15 if you flipped 7 different numbers.
  5. Busted? The whole round scores 0.

Example: 12 + 9 + 7 = 28, doubled by ×2 = 56, then a +4 card makes 60.

The game ends at the end of the round in which someone passes the winning score. Highest total wins.
