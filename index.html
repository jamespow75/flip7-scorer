Scoring a round

  1. Add up your number cards.
  2. Got the ×2 card? Double that number.
  3. Add any +2 to +10 cards. These are never doubled.
  4. Seven different numbers? That's a Flip 7: add 15.
  5. Two of the same number means bust: 0 for the round.

Example: 12 + 9 + 7 = 28. Doubled with ×2 that's 56. A +4 card makes 60.

When someone reaches the winning score, finish the round. The highest total wins. If it's a tie, play another round.
